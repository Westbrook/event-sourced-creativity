## Activity recording

### Record where you focus?

This can help the app learn which parts of your canvases matter to you so it can order suggestions better.

- What this records: which canvas area settles in view and at what zoom, which objects you select, and when you join or leave a canvas.
- What this never records: mouse paths, anything you type, your screen, camera, microphone, or activity while the app is not visible.
- Who can see your choice: a workspace administrator can see whether you enabled recording, but cannot see the recorded detail.
- How long: device-level detail is deleted after 14 days.
- What power it has: it may gently reorder suggestions. It can never create, change, hide, or delete work. A direct action always carries at least four times its authority.
- Your controls: decline, pause, review all retained detail, or delete it.

Declining does not disable live collaboration. Teammates still see your name, cursor, and current selection while you share a canvas; that presence evaporates and is never recorded.

Actions: **Not now** · **Turn on**

## Team-wide suggestions

Team-level learning requires both workspace enablement and each person’s individual opt-in. A contribution joins only an access-scoped daily aggregate of at least five people. Below five, nothing is computed. Administrators can see who opted in but cannot see individual signals.

Leaving deletes retained personal detail and stops future contribution. Past aggregate totals cannot be separated after daily blending; that limit must remain visible at the moment of consent.

Actions: **Not now** · **Count me in**

## Project membership

Adding a person or team grants the selected role over the project’s documents, canvases, blocks, outputs, and complete authored history. It never grants access to another person’s private activity residue, suggestions, or taste profile. Removing membership stops future access; information already seen cannot be unseen.

## Outside images

Before outside pixels enter a generation, the person invoking generation must confirm ownership or a license covering that use. The project history retains the rights receipt and any `influenced-by` relationship.

## Offline custody

Offline changes remain readable on the unlocked device until reconnection, for at most 14 days and within 10,000 operations or 50 MB. Generation and other spending work does not run offline.
