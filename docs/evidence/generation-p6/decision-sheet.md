## Decisive settlement finding

**The existing account path cannot currently satisfy the between-arm gate.** It lacks both an established authoritative final-charge source and the trusted producer connecting that source to P5.

- **Attribution:** after purchase, the original HTTP response provides request identity/usage and Forma binds its receipt hash to the operation ([transport][transport]). That proves the observed response, not a bill. The [Costs API][costs] supplies USD amounts by daily project/key/line-item groups, with no request-ID selector or final/adjustment-complete field. A provider-issued request-level billing assessment, or an authenticated verifier's reconciliation backed by provider billing records and complete exclusive-scope accounting, could substantiate the original purchase; neither is supplied or established for this account.
- **Concurrent charges:** shared project totals cannot allocate overlapping requests. API-key grouping can distinguish other keys; traffic using the same key/line item remains aggregated. The [Images Usage API][usage] offers minute/hour/day request counts, not request-level money. A proven exclusive key/window could assist attribution, but P5's concurrency-one limit controls only P5. Existing-account exclusivity is unknown; choosing a new key or stopping other traffic is not authorized here.
- **Finality and adjustments:** response usage × applicable rates is still an estimate in [transport lines 56–60][transport]. Costs documentation supplies no completion watermark or adjustment-finality guarantee. A stable balance, paid aggregate invoice or owner risk acceptance alone does not establish immutable per-request finality. The [schema finality trigger][schema] forbids decreasing incurred totals and forbids any provider-total change once settled. Thus a provisional value cannot be registered as final and repaired later. The verifier needs a source-backed basis that adjustments for this original are resolved; absent it, uncertainty stays open.
- **Availability:** request/usage evidence can arrive with the original response; costs arrive only after provider accounting reports them. The inspected billing contract promises neither a finality deadline nor availability before **2026-10-01 00:00 UTC**. Account billing cadence and finality evidence are missing. No future receipt is demanded now; what is needed before purchase is a credible, authorized way to obtain and verify it afterward, including lost-ack handling without repurchase.
- **Registration and gate:** only a separately qualified billing verifier acting through trusted server code is an intended reconciliation producer. No actual person/service is qualified by the available records. [Append helper lines 32–59][reconciliation] assumes that qualification and needs an existing immutable charge; no study charge writer connects to it. The separate preparation writer targets `generation_preparation_*`, not this study. [Host lines 193–228][host] requires matching original request/receipt/scope, settled provider liability, zero uncertainty, funded lifecycle, retained artifact and reservations within allowance; [treatment guard line 76][human] separately requires completed baseline and durable clear human observation/resume. Neither financial mode waives these gates. Late evidence can reconcile the original obligation; it cannot authorize expired treatment.
