## Fixed corpus and measurement rules

`tests/fixtures/still-creation-corpus.json` contains 36 organization inputs and 65 query/target cases. Seven organization inputs and twelve retrieval targets/queries are labeled tuning; the remaining cases are held out. Retrieval target families do not cross splits. Exact source spans use UTF-16 offsets and preserve multilingual text, combining marks and emoji. Every organization input records supported source material and forbidden inferences. The original White Album paragraph is included unchanged.

Retrieval includes 38 held-out unambiguous queries, five ambiguous queries and ten no-match queries, plus twelve tuning queries. Five cases require paraphrases with zero token overlap between query and target title/body. Cases distinguish a whole Document from a specific constituent Idea, equal labels with different identities, qualification/negation, missing content, revoked/deleted content and content outside the current Project. The corpus seeds accounts with exactly 64, 1,000 and 10,000 Ideas, including authority canaries. Seeded inventory distractors exercise account size; they do not claim realistic semantic competition.

The corpus is fixed before tuning. Do not tune prompts or ranking against held-out outcomes. Changing held-out inputs or expected identities requires a new corpus version. The helper records a SHA-256 corpus digest with evaluations. Expected identities and split labels are stripped before invoking a search adapter.

`tests/helpers/creation-corpus.mjs` measures independent lexical recall at 20, learned-candidate recall at 20, candidate union recall, conditional reranker recall at three, final recall at three, no-match false positives, exposed unauthorized records, per-profile p95 and missing runs. Ambiguous queries do not inflate the unambiguous denominator. Incomplete, malformed, duplicate or unauthorized results cannot qualify. Mock scores never set the live retrieval quality gate. A provider-backed run with degraded coverage cannot qualify that gate either.

Human adjudication of organizational additions and omissions remains mandatory. Valid spans and schemas cannot prove fidelity. Evaluation must preserve qualifiers, negations, identity distinctions and relationships, and must inspect proposed transformed text rather than checking record count alone.
