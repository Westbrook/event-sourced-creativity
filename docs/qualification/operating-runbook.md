## Incremental repair and retention renewal

These procedures describe the committed implementation; the current quantitative disposition is recorded above and in the release decision. The fixed limits remain healthy edit visibility within **5 minutes**, default renewal within **720 minutes**, renewal before the **actual shorter source/snapshot expiry** where that is earlier, and durable D1/R2/private-body erasure within **15 minutes**. Initial backfill retains its 24-hour criterion. A one-day component has a 144-minute renewal lead. Its assigned expiry is measured individually: backfill and materialization stagger component lifetimes, so the all-cohort completion duration is not the deadline of every component. The observer requires each original body, snapshot, manifest and vector range to renew before its own expiry and reports coverage at the earliest original source deadline separately. Local controlled-clock passes would still leave hosted scheduling, timing, retention and authorization unqualified.

**Eligible text changes.** The fast path starts only from a complete, currently valid canonical snapshot with a matching base content version, generation and owner/shared high-water marks. It accepts at most 32 append-only authoring events, each an eligible typed `BlockPropertyEdited` of `text` with exact Idea and Project identities, or an explicitly foreign `BlockRemoved` whose exact identity is absent from all target snapshots and current/former sources. Shared events must have their required scope; a foreign Project claim must not overlap a known identity in this Project. Other structural events, missing/expired snapshots, inconsistent source identity or other unsupported deltas take canonical replay. A snapshot is a derived certificate, not an independent grant of access: current custody, source/private-retention, generation and database-time lease checks apply again inside each publishing batch. Policy/authority and unsafe historical mutations invalidate the snapshot anchor. A new private removal sidecar must agree with public identity and owner/Project custody metadata before unrelated anchors survive; unknown or conflicting claims poison the affected owner scopes. Later private/public rewrites remain conservative. The 500-Idea withdrawal→next-edit regression verifies the preserved anchor against full canonical replay; this exception does not apply to rehome, creation or other structural changes.

For an eligible delta, inspect the projection cursor's base generation/head and affected identity list. Materialization updates the edited Ideas, their containing Documents and the Project representation. Unchanged records keep exact revisions, body pointers and their original retention deadlines. A whole unchanged shard may carry into the current generation only when every member remains exact, current, complete and privately eligible, with the same representation and valid expiry. Mixed or multipart affected shards follow bounded copy/rebuild work. Do not manually change source versions, membership, publication flags or generation to clear a backlog. Those values must advance through the fenced canonical workflow.

**Suspension and retirement.** A generation change immediately denies previous published sources. When a valid snapshot anchor exists, an active shard may enter `suspended` while the narrow delta is checked; suspension is not searchable coverage. Its deadline is at most **300 seconds** from invalidation, shortened by any earlier deadline and never pushed out by repeated edits. The atomic carry path may return only an exact unchanged, unexpired suspended shard to active state under the new generation. Rejected carry, loss of its anchor or deadline expiry retires it. A retired or expired shard cannot be revived by this path. Current authority loss still denies first and drives cleanup; suspension does not enlarge the 15-minute physical-erasure requirement. If the allowed work cannot finish before suspension expires, expect canonical reconstruction/partial coverage rather than stale results.

**Renew unchanged content before expiry.** Inspect both source-body and snapshot-body assigned expiry, not only the vector manifest or a default retention duration. Renewal becomes due during the last 10% of an assigned lifetime, capped at one day with a 60-second minimum lead. It reauthorizes the current complete generation, validates source identity/content hash and snapshot identity, and creates **new private body records and retention sidecars** before replacing pointers atomically. It never edits an old body's assigned expiry or revives an expired derivative. A snapshot reaching its own deadline triggers renewal even if its source body expires later. Old unreferenced derived bodies remain subject to independent cleanup.

Unchanged renewal needs no embedding/provider call and does not replay the whole Project or increment its generation. A shared vector object's manifest expiry advances only after every member has a current complete source, a valid private lifetime, and verified R2 bytes/checksum; the atomic fence rechecks those facts after I/O. Its new expiry cannot exceed the earliest eligible member lifetime. Partial body renewal leaves a still-valid old shard untouched until all member conditions hold. Record source/snapshot replacement counts, object checks, queue age and the earliest remaining expiry to confirm sustained coverage; do not count merely extending a manifest as successful body renewal.

**Fallback and bounded holds.** Expired private source material, stale/invalid snapshots, malformed derived bodies or corrupt/missing vectors trigger a freshly authorized canonical rebuild. The fallback rechecks the current generation and lease, removes the unsafe snapshot anchor and invalidates the partition; it cannot turn a concurrent edit, lease loss or revocation into write authority. An expired derivative is never used to mint a replacement. The rebuild may require ordinary budgeted embedding calls and may temporarily lose complete coverage, which must remain visible in results and evidence.

Current renewal work is bounded to 32 sources and 1,000,000 combined source/snapshot bytes per quantum, with at most four shard checks and 1 MiB of shard bytes. Existing wall-time, D1, upload and provider-attempt controls still apply. Treat errors such as `index_renewal_body_capacity`, `index_projection_capacity`, `index_container_capacity` or an inconsistent snapshot schema as named operational holds. Preserve source content and failure receipts; correct the diagnosed capacity/schema/authority condition and resume only through the authorized administrative path. Do not silently truncate bodies, relax the deadline, raise paid limits or repeatedly force a generation to make a failing workload appear healthy. A retry, fallback or held job is not proof that renewal completed before the actual expiry.
